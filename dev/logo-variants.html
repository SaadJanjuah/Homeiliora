Wild vines — mixed foliage, irregular growth

Four different leaf shapes instead of one repeated, sizes from 0.6× to 1.3×, angles scattered rather than mirrored, some leaves clustered and some gaps, stems that wander and cross, plus a few aerial roots. Irregularity is what makes it read as a plant instead of a pattern.
